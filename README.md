# Incident Life Cycle Automation in ServiceNow

## Project Overview
Incident Life Cycle Automation in ServiceNow aims to streamline and standardize the incident management process, from incident creation to resolution and closure.

The project focuses on reducing manual effort, improving incident tracking, supporting faster troubleshooting, and improving service quality.

## Problem Statement
Manual incident handling can lead to delays, inconsistent classification, inefficient assignment, and difficulty tracking incident resolution and service-level agreement (SLA) compliance.

## Proposed Solution
The proposed solution uses ServiceNow incident management capabilities to organize and automate the incident lifecycle.

### Key Objectives
- Standardize incident creation and classification.
- Route incidents to appropriate support groups.
- Support troubleshooting through knowledge management.
- Enable escalation for complex incidents.
- Support change requests and child incidents when required.
- Improve incident tracking and resolution visibility.

## Stakeholders
- **End Users:** Report issues and track their resolution.
- **Service Desk Agents:** Create, classify, assign, and manage incidents.
- **Level 2 Support Teams:** Investigate and resolve complex incidents.
- **Change Management:** Manage required changes.
- **ServiceNow Administrators:** Maintain platform configuration.
